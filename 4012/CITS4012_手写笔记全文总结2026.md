# Part A — Word Representation 词表示（10分，Q1 Q2 Q3）

## A.1 One-hot 独热向量  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** one-hot 向量有什么缺点？为什么它看不出词与词的相似度？

**中文：** 独热向量的做法是：词表有多大，向量就多长；这个词对应的位置写1，其他位置全写0。它有四个缺点：第一，看不出语义相似度，两个近义词的独热向量算余弦相似度也是0；第二，向量非常长，计算效率低；第三，向量很稀疏，大部分是0，浪费内存；第四，捕捉不到上下文。
**English:** One-hot vector: the vector length equals the vocabulary size; the word's position is 1 and all other positions are 0. Disadvantages: (1) no **semantic** similarity — cosine similarity of two **synonyms** is 0; (2) very long vectors are inefficient; (3) **sparse** vectors waste memory; (4) no context information.

## A.2 BoW 词袋模型  ★
> **考试可能这样问：** 用 BoW 表示"猫喜欢鱼"，向量长什么样？它丢了什么信息（词序）？

**中文：** 词袋模型统计词频，把所有one-hot向量求和，变成一个整篇文档的向量。它的缺点是：丢掉顺序和上下文语义，分不清陈述和疑问；向量高维稀疏，不理解近义词的相似度。
**English:** Bag-of-words (BoW) counts word frequency and sums all one-hot vectors into one **document vector**. Disadvantages: it loses word order and context (cannot tell statements from questions); the vector is high-dimensional and sparse, and it does not capture similarity between **synonyms**.

## A.3 TF-IDF（2021年Q3a考过手算）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** TF、IDF 各自是干什么的？为什么常见词（the）的 IDF 小？

> **考法（2021 Q3a）：** 给你一个小语料库，挑一个词让你完整算一遍 TF-IDF。例子：vegetables 在文档1出现1次（TF=1），3篇文档里只有1篇有它（IDF=log(3/1)≈1.0986），TF-IDF=1×1.0986=1.0986。**背公式：TF×log(总文档数÷含这个词的文档数)。**

**TF-IDF 给每个词计算一个重要性权重，公式是：$w_{ij} = \text{TF}_{ij} \times \log\left(\frac{N}{\text{df}_i}\right)$。

* **TF（词频）：** 统计一个词在**当前文档**中出现的次数。通常一个词在当前文档里出现越频繁（TF 越高），说明它对这篇文档越重要。

* **IDF（逆文档频率）：** 衡量这个词在**整个语料库**中的通用程度。如果一个词（如 "the"、"is"）在几乎所有文档里都大量出现（文档频率 $\text{df}_i$ 极高），其 $\frac{N}{\text{df}_i}$ 就会接近 1，$\log(1) = 0$，IDF 降为 0。

* **核心平衡逻辑：** TF 负责找出当前文章的高频重点词，而 IDF 负责过滤掉在所有文章中都出现的无意义通用词（如 "the"）。两者相乘后，只有那些**在当前文章频繁出现、但在其他文章中很少见**的代表性特征词，才能拿到高分。

**English:**

TF-IDF computes an importance weight for each word: $w_{ij} = \text{TF}_{ij} \times \log\left(\frac{N}{\text{df}_i}\right)$.

* **TF (Term Frequency):** Counts how often a word appears in the **current document**. A higher frequency usually indicates higher importance for that specific document.

* IDF (**<u>Inverse </u>Document Frequency**): Measures how common or rare a word is across the **entire corpus**. If a common word (e.g., "the", "is") appears in almost every document ($\text{df}_i$ is very large), its $\frac{N}{\text{df}_i}$ becomes close to 1, making its IDF value near 0 ($\log(1) = 0$).

* **Core Balancing Logic:** TF identifies locally frequent words, while IDF penalizes globally common noise words. By multiplying them ($\text{TF} \times \text{IDF}$), only words that are **frequent in the current document but rare across other documents** receive a high final score

**中文（实际例子，考试让"举例子"直接抄）：** ①垃圾邮件检测：把每封邮件转成 TF-IDF 向量（每个词一维=它的 TF-IDF 权重），用带标签的邮件训练分类器（如逻辑回归）。新邮件来了，算它的 TF-IDF 向量，分类器判断垃圾还是正常。为什么好用：垃圾邮件里的"免费、中奖、点击"出现多（TF高）而且在正常邮件里少见（IDF高），权重被抬高，模型一看到这些词就警觉。②搜索引擎排序：用户搜"篮球"，把查询词和每篇文档的 TF-IDF 向量做余弦相似度，按分数从高到低返回。"篮球"在体育文档里 TF-IDF 高所以排前面；每篇都带"the"的通用文档分数低。③关键词提取：一篇文章里 TF-IDF 得分最高的几个词就是它的"主题词"。一句话：TF-IDF 就是"把文本变成数字、让重点词浮出来"的工具，哪都能用。
**English (real examples, copy directly if asked to "give an example"):** ①Spam email detection: convert each email into a TF-IDF vector (one dimension per word = its TF-IDF weight), train a classifier (e.g. logistic regression) on labelled emails. For a new email, compute its TF-IDF vector and the classifier decides spam or not. Why it works: spam words like "free", "win", "click" appear often (high TF) and are rare in normal emails (high IDF), so their weights are boosted — the model becomes alert to them. ②Search engine ranking: when a user searches "basketball", compute cosine similarity between the query and each document's TF-IDF vector and rank by score. "basketball" has a high TF-IDF in sports documents, so they rank first; documents full of "the" score low. ③Keyword extraction: the words with the highest TF-IDF scores in an article are its topic keywords. In short: TF-IDF turns text into numbers and lets important words stand out.

### 【样题 Q1 · 3分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** TF-IDF为什么要引入IDF因素？举一个具体例子。
**英文答案（English answer):** TF only counts how often a word appears in one document. Common words like "the" and "is" appear everywhere but carry little information. IDF lowers the weight of these common words and raises the weight of rare informative words. Example: in a sports article, "the" appears 50 times but its IDF is near 0, so its TF-IDF score is low; "basketball" appears only in sports documents, its IDF is high, so its score is high. Thus IDF helps identify the real topic.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** TF只统计一个词在文档里出现几次。the这类词到处都有但没用。IDF降低通用词权重、抬高稀有代表词权重。例子：体育文章里the出现50次但IDF接近0，得分低；basketball只在体育文档出现，IDF高，得分高。所以IDF帮我们识别文档真正主题。

## A.4 Word2Vec（词向量/词嵌入）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** Word2Vec 是静态还是动态词向量？CBOW 和 Skip-gram 各预测什么？

**中文：** Word2Vec把词变成低维度的稠密向量，是词的分布式表示。它解决了独热向量的缺陷：维度固定且低、计算效率高、能表示词义相似度。背后的假设是：语义、句法相近的词，在高维空间里位置互相靠近。它有两种结构：CBOW拿周围上下文词预测中间中心词；Skip-gram拿中心词预测周围上下文词。训练是无监督的。
**English:** Word2Vec maps words to low-dimensional dense vectors — a distributed representation. It fixes one-hot's problems: fixed low dimension, higher efficiency, and **semantic** similarity. The assumption: words with similar meaning or **syntax** are close together in the vector space. Two architectures: CBOW predicts the center word from the context words; Skip-gram predicts the context words from the center word. Training is unsupervised.

### 【样题 Q2 · 3分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** 什么是词类比任务？为什么能用来评估词向量？举例。
**英文答案（English answer）：** A word analogy task tests whether a model captures **semantic**and **syntactic** relationships using **vector arithmetic**, in the form "A is to B as C is to D" (v_A − v_B ≈ v_C − v_D). Good word embeddings map similar words close together and keep linear relations. If the model completes the analogy correctly, it proves the embedding learned meaningful concepts, not random numbers. Example: King − Man + Woman ≈ Queen.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）： 词类比任务用向量加减测试词的语义和句法关系，形式是"A对B等于C对D"。好的词向量把近义词放得近、保留线性关系。模型能答对类比，说明embedding学到的是有意义的概念，不是随机数。例子：国王−男人+女人≈女王。

## A.5 CBOW 细节 & 负采样（2021年考过，背这个版本）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 负采样为什么用？正样本对、负样本对怎么构造？（2021 考过）

**中文：** CBOW的过程：每个单词变成one-hot，乘矩阵W得到向量v，把所有v加起来求平均，再乘矩阵做softmax得到概率，用交叉熵做损失，梯度下降更新矩阵。
**English:** CBOW: each word becomes one-hot, multiplied by matrix W to get v, all v's are averaged, then multiplied again and passed through softmax; the loss is cross-entropy, updated by gradient descent.

**中文（负采样·考法版，和2021年Q3c同款）：** 负采样是Word2Vec（CBOW和Skip-gram共用）训练时的加速技巧：正常softmax要算整个词表的概率，几万个词太慢；负采样只挑1个正确样本加2到5个错误样本代入损失计算。具体做法：用滑动窗口提取真实的"中心词-上下文词"对当正样本（标签1），再从词表随机抽没出现在窗口的词当负样本（标签0），训练一个二分类器，最大化正样本概率、最小化负样本概率。
**English (exam version, same as 2021 Q3c):** Negative sampling is a speed-up trick in Word2Vec training (shared by CBOW and Skip-gram): full softmax over the whole vocabulary is too slow, so we pick 1 positive sample plus 2–5 negative samples for the loss. Process: extract true center-context word pairs with a sliding window as positive pairs (label 1); randomly sample words NOT in the window as negative pairs (label 0); train a binary classifier that maximises the probability of positive pairs and minimises it for negative pairs.

**中文（选负样本公式+局限，笔记第3页原样）：** 选负样本按词频概率抽：`P(wᵢ)=f(wᵢ)/Σf(wⱼ)`（该词词频÷全部词总词频），因为高频词经常出现、容易混淆。局限：①无法体现词形相同性（如词根词）；②低频词训练出的向量差；③语料库外的词无法处理（词频未知）。
**English (sampling formula + limitations, from the notes):** Negative samples are drawn by frequency: `P(wᵢ)=f(wᵢ)/Σf(wⱼ)` (word frequency divided by total frequency), because high-frequency words appear often and are easy to confuse. Limitations: (1) cannot capture word-shape similarity (e.g. word roots); (2) low-frequency words get poor vectors; (3) out-of-vocabulary words cannot be handled because their frequency is unknown.

## A.6 FastText（了解即可）  ★
> **考试可能这样问：** FastText 和 Word2Vec 有什么区别？靠什么解决生词问题？

**中文：** FastText把一个词切成字符n-gram（比如Apple切成Ap, pp, pl, le），这样稀有词和语料库外的词也能得到向量。
**English:** FastText splits a word into character n-grams (e.g. Apple → Ap, pp, pl, le), so rare words and out-of-vocabulary words still get vectors.

## A.7 GloVe（2021年Q3d考过）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** GloVe 基于什么（共现矩阵）？和 Word2Vec 有什么区别？

> **考法（2021 Q3d）：** 解释"GloVe 怎么解决 Word2Vec 的局限"。答题话术：Word2Vec 只用滑动窗口扫局部上下文，丢掉了全语料库的全局共现统计；GloVe 直接在全局共现矩阵上训练，既保留全局统计关系，又保持词向量预测快。

**中文：** GloVe用全局共现统计来计算条件概率（比如"冰"这个词更接近"固态"还是"水蒸气"）。它解决了Word2Vec只看局部上下文的局限，因为GloVe关心全文的全局信息。它的局限是分不清上位词（fruit）和下位词（apple），而且不同语料库训练出的向量不同。
**English:** GloVe uses global co-occurrence statistics to compute conditional probabilities (e.g. whether "ice" is closer to "solid" or "gas"). It fixes Word2Vec's local-window limitation by using global **corpus** information. Limitations: it cannot distinguish hypernyms (fruit) from hyponyms (apple), and vectors differ across **corpora**.

## A.8 上下文词嵌入（ELMo / BERT 思想）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 同一个词在不同句子里向量一样吗？上下文嵌入解决了静态嵌入的什么问题？（样题 Q3）

**中文：** 上下文词嵌入的核心是：同一个词，放在不同句子里，向量不一样，因为它会读周围的语境。比如bank在"存钱进银行"里是银行，在"坐在河岸上"里是河岸。Word2Vec是静态嵌入，训练完bank只有一套固定向量；ELMo是动态上下文嵌入，bank在不同句子里向量不同。ELMo的生成方式：用预训练双向LSTM，单词先拿基础词嵌入，整句进双向LSTM拿每层隐状态，对多层隐状态做加权拼接求和得到最终表征。
**English:** **Contextual embeddings**: the same word gets different vectors in different sentences because the model reads the context. Example: in "deposit money in the bank" bank means a financial bank; in "sit beside the river bank" it means the river side. Word2Vec is static — bank has one fixed vector; ELMo is contextual — bank changes with context. ELMo uses a pretrained bidirectional LSTM: the word gets a base embedding, the sentence goes through the bi-LSTM, and the hidden states from multiple layers are weighted and combined into the final representation.

### 【样题 Q3 · 4分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** 要区分两句话里"Washington"的不同表示，选哪种嵌入技术？
**英文答案（English answer）：** Use **contextualized** word embeddings such as BERT or ELMo. Static embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe) give only one fixed vector for "Washington", treating the state and the person the same. Contextualized embeddings change the vector based on context. In sentence 1, BERT sees "state", "West Coast", "populous" → vector means a geographical state. In sentence 2, it sees "George", "officer", "president" → vector means a person. So the vector changes with context.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 选上下文词向量（BERT或ELMo）。静态嵌入只给Washington一个固定向量，把州名和人名当成同一个。上下文嵌入会根据语境变化。句1看到state/West Coast/populous，向量代表地理州；句2看到George/officer/president，向量代表人。所以向量随语境变化。

## A.9 词向量评估（大纲第3周）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 词向量怎么评估？类比任务（king−man+woman）测的是什么能力？

**中文：** 词向量有两种评估方式。内在评估：在特定任务上直接测词向量本身的质量，比如词类比任务，计算快，能帮助理解系统。外在评估：放到真实下游任务上测准确率，花时间，但出问题时很难分清是算法、方法相互作用、还是数据的问题。评估分两个层面：语义semantic和句法syntactic。
**English:** Two ways to evaluate word embeddings. **Intrinsic** evaluation: test the vectors directly on a specific task (e.g. word analogy); it is fast and helps understand the system. **Extrinsic** evaluation: measure accuracy on a real downstream task; it is time-consuming, and when it fails it is hard to tell whether the problem is the algorithm, the interaction of methods, or the data. Two levels: semantic and syntactic.

## A.10 机器学习基础（大纲第3周，2021年Q2考过）  ★
> **考试可能这样问：** softmax 为什么要用？交叉熵和 MSE 各用在哪？batch/SGD/mini-batch 区别？（2021 Q2）

> **考法（2021 Q2 + 大纲第3周考点）：** ①softmax 手算（给一行 logits 算概率）；②负对数似然 NLL 损失 = 真实类别的概率取负对数 L=-log(p_correct)，预测越准损失越小；③minibatch 损失 = 这一批所有样本 NLL 取平均；④batch 和 SGD 优缺点对比（batch 稳但慢费内存，SGD 快能跳出局部最小但波动大）。**⑤大纲第3周明确考点："为什么需要反向传播更新参数"——答案就是下方【反向传播】那一段。**

**中文：** 激活函数：sigmoid把数值压到0到1之间，可导，做门控；tanh压到-1到1；ReLU负数置0、正数保留；GELU是Transformer和BERT用的；SwiGLU是最新大模型的标配。损失函数：MSE均方误差用于回归，交叉熵用于分类，分类要先过softmax让概率和为1。梯度下降：batch最慢，SGD每条数据更新最快但波动大，mini-batch折中，最好用Adam。
**English:** Activation functions: sigmoid maps values to (0,1), differentiable, used for gating; tanh maps to (−1,1); ReLU keeps positives and zeroes negatives; GELU is used in Transformers and BERT; SwiGLU is the standard in modern LLMs. Loss functions: MSE is for regression; cross-entropy is for classification, and softmax is applied first so probabilities sum to 1. Gradient descent: batch is slow; SGD updates per example (fast but noisy); mini-batch is a compromise; Adam is the best improvement.

**中文（补：反向传播为什么需要，大白话）：** 训练分两步走。前向传播：数据从输入一路算到输出，得到预测，和标准答案一比算出损失（错得有多大）。反向传播：从损失出发往回走，用链式法则算出每一个参数该往哪调、调多少（这个方向加大小就是梯度）。最后梯度下降更新：参数=参数−学习率×梯度，损失就变小一点。没有反向传播，参数永远是随机数，模型学不会。反向传播负责"算梯度"，梯度下降负责"拿梯度更新"，两个是配套的。
**English (why backpropagation is needed, plain words):** Training has two passes. Forward: data flows input→output, we get the prediction and compare it with the answer to compute the loss (how wrong we are). Backward: starting from the loss, we go back and use the chain rule to compute a gradient for every parameter — the direction and size of the update. Then gradient descent updates: parameter = parameter − learning rate × gradient, so the loss decreases a little. Without backpropagation, parameters stay random and the model can never learn. Backpropagation computes the gradients; gradient descent uses them to update — the two work together.

**中文（大纲串联点，必背）：** sigmoid的门控思想一路用到底——LSTM三扇门用sigmoid把数值压到0到1，接近0就过滤丢弃、接近1就完整保留；SwiGLU也继续用这套门控思路。另外，多层感知机MLP就是Transformer里的逐位置前馈网络FFN，"逐位置"很关键，FFN独立作用于序列里的每一个token。
**English (syllabus connection point):** The sigmoid gating idea runs through everything — the three LSTM gates use sigmoid to compress values to (0,1): near 0 means discard, near 1 means keep; SwiGLU uses the same gating idea. Also, MLP corresponds to the position-wise feed-forward network (FFN) in the Transformer; "position-wise" is key — FFN acts independently on each token.

# Part B — Sequence Models and NLP Applications 序列模型与NLP应用（10分，Q4 Q5 Q6）

## B.1 NLP 任务分类 & 预处理（了解）  ★
> **考试可能这样问：** NLP 有哪些任务？预处理有哪些步骤？词形还原和词干提取有什么区别？

## B.2 RNN 循环神经网络  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** RNN 为什么能处理序列？隐状态 h_t 是怎么更新的？

**中文：** RNN是带记忆的循环网络，公式是`h_t = tanh(W_hh·h_{t-1} + W_xh·x_t + b_h)`，不用死记，理解逻辑：当前输入投影加前一步隐状态投影，相加后套tanh。RNN反向传播开销大，改进办法是按时间截断反向传播，向前算N步就更新一次权重。
**English:** RNN is a **recurrent** network with memory: `h_t = tanh(W_hh·h_{t-1} + W_xh·x_t + b_h)`.  project the input, project the previous hidden state, add them, apply tanh. **Backpropagation** through time is expensive; the fix is truncated BPTT — update weights every N steps.

**中文（大纲第4周必背串联点）：** 为什么RNN不需要显式位置编码？因为RNN按顺序逐个处理token，隐状态天然携带时序顺序信息。而Transformer移除了循环结构，自注意力分不清词序，所以必须额外加显式位置编码。
**English (syllabus point):** Why doesn't RNN need **explicit** positional encoding? Because RNN processes tokens one by one in order, so the hidden state naturally carries order information. The Transformer removes recurrence; self-attention cannot tell word order, so explicit positional encoding must be added.

## B.3 RNN 的缺陷（2021年Q5a考过梯度消失）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** RNN 为什么学不了长距离依赖？梯度消失的三个原因是什么？（2021 Q5a）

> **考法（2021 Q5a）：** 解释标准RNN（Elman RNN）在序列建模里有什么缺陷。答题话术：回传时梯度一路相乘会消失（梯度消失），抓不住长距离依赖；解决办法就是加门控（LSTM/GRU）。

**中文：** RNN有三个缺陷：第一，信息衰减，长序列里开头离末尾太远，早期信息占比不断衰减；第二，向前计算本身也丢信息；第三，梯度消失和梯度爆炸，回溯时不断和权重相乘，权重小则梯度趋近0（早期位置学不动），权重大则数值爆炸（找不到最优）。解决办法是门机制。
**English:** RNN has three problems: (1) information decay — in long sequences the early information keeps **shrinking**; (2) forward computation itself loses information; (3) **vanishing and exploding** gradients — multiplying by small weights drives gradients to 0 (early positions stop learning), large weights **blow up** the values. The solution is gating.

**中文（为什么梯度会消失·大纲第4周要求复习原理，大白话）：** 三个原因叠在一起：①RNN 每一步共用同一组权重 W_hh，展开后是一条很长很长的链；②反向传播用链式法则，损失对第1步的梯度要一路乘回来，每过一步都乘一个"权重×tanh的导数"，而 tanh 的导数最大才1、平时都小于1，权重也常小于1；③小于1的数连乘几百次就指数缩小到0，所以句子开头的词收不到梯度、学不动——这就是梯度消失。长距离依赖问题就是它造成的：开头的词影响不到结尾的预测，模型记不住远距离信息。梯度爆炸同理：因子平均大于1就指数爆炸；消失比爆炸更常见，因为 tanh/sigmoid 的导数都在0~1之间。解决办法：门控（LSTM/GRU 用加性通路，让梯度不用每次都乘一个小于1的数）。
**English (why gradients vanish, required by Week 4 syllabus, plain words):** Three reasons stack up: (1) RNN shares one weight matrix W_hh at every step; when unrolled, it is a very long chain. (2) By the chain rule, the gradient for step 1 is a product over all steps — each step multiplies by (weight × tanh derivative), and the tanh derivative is at most 1, usually < 1; weights are also often < 1. (3) A number < 1 multiplied hundreds of times shrinks exponentially to 0, so early tokens get no gradient and cannot learn — vanishing gradients. This is why long-range dependencies fail: words at the start cannot affect predictions at the end. Exploding gradients are the same story when the factors average > 1; vanishing is more common because tanh/sigmoid derivatives lie in (0,1). The fix is gating: LSTM/GRU add an additive path so gradients do not have to be multiplied by a small number at every step.

## B.4 LSTM（解决梯度消失）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** LSTM 三扇门各干什么？为什么它能记住长距离信息？（样题 Q5）

**中文：** LSTM用的操作：sigmoid做门控开关（0到1），tanh调整数值范围，逐元素相乘过滤信息，逐元素相加更新记忆，向量拼接结合上一步隐状态和当前输入。它的参数量是RNN的四倍，通过门控解决梯度消失，能更好捕捉长期依赖。
**English:** LSTM operations: sigmoid gates (0–1), tanh scaling, **pointwise** multiplication to filter, pointwise addition to update memory, **concatenation** to combine the previous hidden state and current input. It has 4× more parameters than RNN, solves vanishing gradients with gating, and captures long-term dependencies better.

### 【样题 Q5 · 4分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** 标出LSTM里三个门的位置，分别解释功能和目的。
**英文答案（English answer）：** 1) Forget gate (f_t): uses sigmoid to output 0–1, decides what information to **discard** from the previous cell state C_{t-1}. 2) Input gate (i_t): combines a sigmoid layer (which values to update) and a tanh layer (creates new candidate C̃_t), decides what new information to store into cell state C_t. 3) Output gate (o_t): sigmoid decides which parts of cell state to output, scaled by tanh, decides the next hidden state h_t.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 遗忘门用sigmoid输出0到1，决定从旧细胞状态丢弃哪些信息；输入门用sigmoid决定更新哪些值、用tanh生成新候选值，决定存哪些新信息进细胞状态；输出门用sigmoid决定细胞状态哪些部分输出，经tanh缩放，决定下一个隐藏状态。

## B.5 GRU（2021年Q5a考过：怎么解决RNN缺陷）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** GRU 比 LSTM 少了什么？两个门各干什么？（2021 Q5a）

> **考法（2021 Q5a）：** 解释GRU怎么解决标准RNN的梯度消失。答题话术：标准RNN梯度一路相乘会消失、抓不住长距离依赖；GRU加了重置门（决定忘多少过去）和更新门（决定带多少旧状态），还有加性的记忆通路，让梯度能顺畅流过长序列。

**中文：** GRU去掉输出门，比LSTM轻量。它有两个门：更新门决定保留多少旧信息、输入多少新信息；重置门决定忘记多少过去的上下文。更新公式：`h_t = (1−Z_t)h_{t-1} + Z_t h̃_t`，是旧状态和新记忆的加权平均。
**English:** GRU removes the output gate and is lighter than LSTM. Two gates: the update gate decides how much old information to keep and new information to add; the reset gate decides how much past context to forget. Update: `h_t = (1−Z_t)h_{t-1} + Z_t h̃_t` — a weighted average of the old state and the new candidate memory.

## B.6 双向 RNN Bi-RNN（2021年Q4b、Q5b考过BiLSTM）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 为什么用双向？两个方向的隐状态怎么合并成最终输出？（样题 Q4）

> **考法（2021 Q4b/Q5b）：** 用BiLSTM当特征提取器或做编码器。答题话术：正反两个方向都读，每个位置都有双向上下文；因为情感分析和词序、否定词关系很大（not good vs good），词袋会丢顺序，BiLSTM能保留顺序依赖。

**中文：** 单向RNN只从左到右读，每个词只看过去信息。双向RNN正反两个方向都读，每个位置既能看过去也能看未来。正向和反向向量拼接会得到更丰富的表示（维度翻倍）；相加则维度不变但会损失信息。
**English:** A vanilla RNN reads only left to right, so each word uses only past information. A Bi-RNN reads both directions, so each position uses both past and future context. **Concatenating** forward and backward vectors gives a richer representation (double dimension); adding them keeps the dimension but loses some information.

### 【样题 Q4 · 3分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** 双向RNN比普通单向RNN好在哪？举例。
**英文答案（English answer）：** A vanilla RNN reads text only left to right, so each word only uses past information. A Bi-directional RNN reads both directions and uses both past and future context at any position. Example: fill-in-the-blank "He was feeling ___ because he lost his keys". Vanilla RNN only sees "He was feeling" and cannot guess; Bi-RNN also sees "because he lost his keys" and easily predicts "upset" or "sad".
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 普通单向RNN只从左往右读，每个词只看得到过去。双向RNN正反两个方向读，任何位置都能用过去加未来的上下文。例子：填空"He was feeling ___ because he lost his keys"，单向只看到"He was feeling"猜不出，双向还能看到后面的原因，容易猜出upset或sad。

## B.7 seq2seq 与任务类型（2021年Q5b考过编解码器）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 编码器、解码器各干什么？只用最后一步隐状态有什么问题？（2021 Q5b）

> **考法（2021 Q5b）：** 解释"BiLSTM编码器+单向LSTM解码器"怎么工作。答题话术：编码器双向读完整句话，把前后向最终状态拼成一个上下文向量φ；φ（经线性变换）当解码器初始状态；解码器从BOS开始一步步自回归生成，每步用上一步预测的词和隐藏状态，softmax输出词表概率，到EOS停止。

**中文：** seq2seq是编码器读输入、解码器生成输出的结构，用在对话和机器翻译。任务类型：多对多是NER和词性标注，多对一是文本分类。最后一层接softmax，把输出转成和为1的概率分布，取概率最大的作为预测标签。
**English:** Seq2seq: the encoder reads the input, the decoder generates the output; used for dialogue and machine translation. Task types: many-to-many for NER and POS tagging; many-to-one for text classification. The final layer uses softmax to turn outputs into probabilities summing to 1; the label with the highest probability is the prediction.

## B.8 评价指标（大纲第5周）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** Precision 的大坑是什么（预测太少也能满分）？F1 怎么算？（样题 Q6）

**中文：** TP是预测正、实际也正；FP是预测正、实际负；TN是预测负、实际负；FN是预测负、实际正。精确率Precision等于TP除以TP加FP，看判为正的里面有多少是真的；召回率Recall等于TP除以TP加FN，看真的正里捞出来多少；F1是精确率和召回率的调和平均；准确率Accuracy等于TP加TN除以全部样本；特异度等于TN除以TN加FP。
**English:** TP: predicted positive, actually positive. FP: predicted positive, actually negative. TN: predicted negative, actually negative. FN: predicted negative, actually positive. Precision = TP/(TP+FP): of all predicted positives, how many are real. Recall = TP/(TP+FN): of all real positives, how many are found. F1 = 2PR/(P+R): harmonic mean. Accuracy = (TP+TN)/(TP+TN+FP+FN): correct over all samples. Specificity = TN/(TN+FP).

### 【样题 Q6 · 3分 · 必背答案（大坑）】

**问题（Question）：** 系统预测10条假新闻，专家核实9假1真。Harry说"我的系统达到90%准确率"。选正确答案。
**英文答案（English answer）：** Correct choice: (b) No, your system achieves 90% Precision. Positive = fake news. TP=9 (predicted fake, actually fake), FP=1 (predicted fake, actually true), total predicted fake = 10. Precision = TP/(TP+FP) = 9/10 = 90%. We cannot compute Accuracy or Recall because we don't know the total number of news, so we don't know TN or FN.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 正确选项是(b)，你的系统达到的是90%精确率。正例是假新闻，TP=9、FP=1、预测为假的总数是10。精确率=9/10=90%。我们不知道新闻总条数，没有TN和FN，所以算不出准确率和召回率。记住：只知道预测为正的数据，永远算不出Accuracy。

> **考试可能这样问：** 情感分类的流程是什么？NER 用什么标注体系（BIO）？（2021 Q4）

> **考法（2021 Q4a）：** 用一层隐藏层的前馈神经网络做观点分类（赞成/中立/反对）——输入是词向量求平均（如300维），过线性层+ReLU，输出层映射到3类，交叉熵损失+Adam训练。Q4b再让你选BiLSTM或1D-CNN当特征提取器，理由见B.6的考法。

**中文：** 情感分析的难点是讽刺检测和多极性。分析要素包括目标对象、属性、态度持有者、态度类型和时间。特征融合方式有拼接、逐元素相加、拼接投影、门控。NER定位文本中的实体并分类，评估依据是实体片段而不是token，常用BIO标注（B是词头，I是词腰，O是词外）。NLP语言层次：音系形态、词法句法、语义、语用。

NER locates and classifies entities; it is evaluated by entity spans, not tokens, and often uses BIO tagging (B=beginning, I=inside, O=outside). NLP levels: phonology/morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics.

## C.1 为什么引入注意力（大纲第6周，2021年Q5c考过）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 早期 seq2seq 的瓶颈是什么？注意力怎么解决它？（2021 Q5c）

> **考法（2021 Q5c）：** 解释注意力怎么改进翻译质量。答题话术：没有注意力时，整句被压成一个固定向量φ，长句子信息不够用（瓶颈）；有了注意力，保留编码器全部隐状态，解码每一步算解码状态和所有编码状态的相关性得分，softmax成权重，加权求和得到动态上下文向量，解码器能盯住源句的关键词（比如翻译emoji时盯着"fire"）。

**中文：** 早期编码器-解码器只把编码器最后一步的隐状态传给解码器，这是瓶颈。注意力机制解决这个问题：解码器每一步都能直接看编码器的每一个token。计算流程：解码器隐状态和各个编码器隐状态做点积，softmax得到注意力权重，权重乘以编码器隐状态加权求和得到注意力输出，再拼接解码器隐状态用来生成下一个token。特殊标记：BOS表示句子开始，EOS表示句子结束，BERT里还有CLS和SEP。
**English:** Early encoder-decoder only passed the encoder's last hidden state to the decoder — a bottleneck. Attention fixes it: at every step the decoder can directly attend to every encoder token. Process: dot-product between the decoder state and encoder states → softmax gives attention weights → weighted sum of encoder states gives the attention output → concatenate with the decoder state to generate the next token. Special tokens: BOS marks the start, EOS marks the end; BERT uses CLS and SEP.

## C.2 Seq2seq with attention 公式（必背）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 注意力三步怎么算（分数→softmax→加权求和）？上下文向量 a_t 是什么？

**中文：** 设编码器隐状态是h1到hn，解码器隐状态是s_t。第一步算注意力分数，s_t和每个h做点积；第二步softmax归一化成概率分布；第三步加权求和得到上下文向量a_t；第四步把a_t和s_t拼接送进网络，输出概率最高的词，再作为下一步输入。
**English:** Let encoder states be h₁…hₙ and the decoder state be s_t. Step 1: attention scores e^t = [s_tᵀh₁, …, s_tᵀhₙ]. Step 2: attention distribution α^t = softmax(e^t). Step 3: context vector a_t = Σ αᵢᵗ hᵢ. Step 4: concatenate [a_t; s_t], feed into the network, output the most probable word as the next input.

## C.3 Attention 的好处  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 注意力带来哪些好处？为什么翻译长句更准？

**中文：** 注意力有四个好处：提高性能，让解码器聚焦源输入的关键部分；解决瓶颈，让解码器直接看源数据；缓解梯度消失，给远处的状态提供捷径；提供可解释性，检查注意力分布就能知道解码器在关注什么。
**English:** Four benefits: better performance (the decoder focuses on key source parts); solves the bottleneck (the decoder sees the source directly); eases vanishing gradients (a shortcut for distant states); interpretability (inspect attention to see what the decoder focuses on).

## C.4 注意力的多种计算方式（了解）  ★
> **考试可能这样问：** 注意力分数有哪些算法（点积/加性等）？各有什么特点？

**中文：** 点积只是注意力的一种计算方式。还有余弦相似度、缩放点积（除以根号d，Transformer用，防止数值过大梯度溢出）、加性注意力、双线性注意力、基于位置的注意力。
**English:** Dot product is just one way to compute attention. Others: cosine similarity, scaled dot-product (divide by √d, used by Transformer to prevent large values), additive, bilinear/general, and location-based attention.

## C.5 全局 vs 局部注意力  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 全局和局部注意力的区别？局部注意力怎么选窗口？（样题 Q7）

**中文：** 全局注意力在生成每个目标词时看输入序列的全部token，计算贵，但能抓住长距离依赖。局部注意力只看预测位置附近的一小段窗口，便宜，但牺牲远距离信息。
**English:** Global attention attends to ALL source tokens when generating each target token — high computation cost, but captures long-range dependencies. Local attention attends only to a small window near the predicted position — cheap, but loses distant information.

### 【样题 Q7 · 4分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** 全局注意力和局部注意力的区别？哪个任务全局更好？
**英文答案（English answer）：** Global attention attends to ALL source tokens when generating each target token → high computation cost. Local attention attends only to a small window/subset of source tokens → lower cost. global More suitable **scenario**: document-level machine translation or abstractive text summarization. Reason: in long documents, key information or coreferences can be far apart (e.g. a pronoun in the last sentence refers to an entity in the first sentence). Global attention covers the whole text and does not miss long-range dependencies.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）： 全局注意力生成每个目标词时看全部输入token，计算贵；局部注意力只看附近一小段窗口，便宜。更适合文档级机器翻译或生成式文本摘要。理由：长文档里关键信息或指代可能相隔很远，比如最后一句的代词指第一句的实体。全局注意力覆盖全文、不漏长距离依赖。

## C.6 self-attention & 阅读理解模型（了解）  ★
> **考试可能这样问：** 自注意力和普通注意力的区别（Q/K/V 都来自哪里）？

**中文：** 自注意力是Q、K、V都来自同一个输入序列，编码器里用它理解多义词的不同范围。Bi-LSTM阅读理解用双路输入（文档和问题）。Bi-DAF用文档和问题的隐状态算相关矩阵，按行softmax看文档对问题的注意力，按列softmax看问题对文档的注意力，双向视角融合。
**English:** Self-attention: Q, K, V all come from the same input sequence; in the encoder it helps understand polysemy in different scopes. Bi-LSTM reading comprehension uses two inputs (document and question). Bi-DAF computes a relevance matrix between document and question hidden states, with row softmax (document→question attention) and column softmax (question→document attention).

## C.7 Transformer 整体架构（大纲第7周）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 编码器、解码器各有哪些模块？数据怎么流（输入到输出）？（样题 Q10 串联）

**中文：** 编码器：输入变成embedding，加位置编码，过多头注意力，加残差和归一化，过前馈网络，再加残差和归一化。解码器：输出变成embedding，加位置编码，先过掩码多头注意力，再过交叉多头注意力，过前馈网络，最后线性层加softmax输出概率。
**English:** Encoder: inputs → embedding → positional encoding → multi-head attention → add & norm → feed forward → add & norm. Decoder: outputs → embedding → positional encoding → masked multi-head attention → add & norm → cross multi-head attention → add & norm → feed forward → add & norm → linear → softmax.

## C.8 注意力通俗类比（必背）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** Q、K、V 各是什么（用检索类比）？多头注意力为什么用多套？（样题 Q9）

**中文：** Query是你的查询，Key是数据库里的标签，Value是真正的内容。流程：Q去匹配一堆K算出权重分数，softmax归一化，再加权求和取出V。自注意力就是输入分别投影三套权重矩阵得到Q、K、V；多头注意力用多套QKV投影，学习不同特征。
**English:** Query is what you look for, Key is the label in the database, Value is the real content. Process: Q matches against K to get scores, softmax normalises them, then the weighted sum gives V. Self-attention projects the input into Q, K, V with three weight matrices; multi-head attention uses several sets of QKV projections to learn different features.

### 【样题 Q9 · 3分 · 必背答案（三处多头注意力）】

**问题（Question）：** Transformer编码器-解码器里多头注意力用在哪些地方，功能有何不同？
**英文答案（English answer）：** 1) Encoder self-attention: inside the encoder. Q, K, V all come from the input sequence. Tokens attend to all other input tokens to build contextual representations. 2) Masked decoder self-attention: inside the decoder. Q, K, V come from previous target tokens; masking blocks future positions so it cannot peek at future words during generation. 3) Encoder-decoder cross-attention: between encoder and decoder. Query comes from the previous decoder layer, Key and Value come from the encoder output. It helps the decoder focus on relevant parts of the input while generating.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 编码器自注意力在编码器内部，Q/K/V全来自输入序列，token互相关注构建上下文表示；掩码解码器自注意力在解码器内部，Q/K/V来自之前的target词，加mask挡住未来位置，生成时不能偷看未来词；交叉注意力在编码器和解码器之间，Q来自解码器前一层，K/V来自编码器输出，帮助解码器生成时聚焦源输入的相关部分。

## C.9 缩放点积注意力  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 为什么要除以 √d_k（缩放）？给数值手算 softmax 权重？（样题 Q8 计算题）

**中文：** 缩放点积注意力的公式是softmax(QK转置除以根号dk)再乘V。为什么除以根号dk：dk很大时点积数值爆炸，softmax进入梯度很小的区域，训练不稳定，缩放一下梯度就稳了。
**English:** Scaled dot-product attention: softmax(QKᵀ/√d_k)V. Why divide by √d_k: when d_k is large, dot products become huge and push softmax into a tiny-gradient region, making training unstable; scaling stabilises the gradients.

### 【样题 Q8 · 4分 · 必背答案（计算题）】

**问题（Question）：** softmax前注意力分数S=[4,2,1]，d_k=4。(a)算缩放分数和注意力权重；(b)为什么用缩放？
**英文答案（English answer）：** (a) √d_k = √4 = 2. Scaled scores S' = [4/2, 2/2, 1/2] = [2, 1, 0.5]. Softmax: e²≈7.389, e¹≈2.718, e^0.5≈1.649, sum≈11.756. Weights ≈ [7.389/11.756, 2.718/11.756, 1.649/11.756] = [0.628, 0.231, 0.140]. (b) When d_k is large, dot products become huge, pushing softmax into a region with very small gradients (vanishing gradient), making training slow or unstable. Scaling by 1/√d_k stabilises the gradients during backpropagation.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 根号dk等于2，缩放后分数是[2, 1, 0.5]。做softmax：e²≈7.389、e¹≈2.718、e^0.5≈1.649，总和约11.756，权重约等于[0.628, 0.231, 0.140]。dk很大时点积数值巨大，softmax进入梯度极小区域，训练慢或不稳，除以根号dk让反向传播时梯度稳定。

## C.10 Transformer 组件（大纲第7周）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 位置编码为什么需要？残差连接干什么？掩码多头注意力怎么算？（样题 Q10）

**中文：** 位置编码用正余弦周期数值把位置归一化，合成唯一的位置向量，让模型知道词的顺序。残差连接是x过函数F再加回x，保证信息完整传输，防止多层丢失底层信息。FFN前馈网络先升维再降维，逐位置独立作用于每个token。掩码多头注意力把后面的词变成负无穷，让未来词对前面词没有贡献。
**English:** Positional encoding uses sinusoidal periodic values to normalise positions into a unique vector, so the model knows word order. Residual connection: x → F(x) + x, keeping information flowing and preventing loss of low-level info in deep networks. FFN expands then reduces the dimension, acting independently per token. Masked multi-head attention sets future positions to −∞ so future words contribute nothing to earlier ones.

**中文（掩码的作用 + 掩码多头注意力如何计算，大纲第7周考点）：** 掩码的作用：解码器生成第 t 个词时，只能看已经生成的 1 到 t-1 个词，不能偷看还没生成的未来词——否则等于作弊，直接抄答案。做法：算完注意力分数后，把矩阵的右上三角（未来位置）全部设成负无穷 −∞；softmax 时 e^(−∞)=0，未来词的注意力权重变 0，对当前词零贡献。计算步骤：①Q/K/V 来自解码器已有的 token 序列（多头就分成多套投影）；②算分数 S=QKᵀ/√d_k（缩放点积）；③加掩码：右上三角设 −∞，对角线和左下保留；④softmax 归一化，未来位置权重变 0；⑤加权求和取 V；⑥多头各算一遍再拼接，过一层线性投影；⑦后面接残差+归一化。注意：掩码只加在**解码器自注意力**；编码器自注意力不用（整句都能看），交叉注意力也不用（源输入本来就是完整的）。
**English (purpose + how masked multi-head attention is computed, Week 7):** Purpose: when the decoder generates the t-th token it may only look at tokens 1..t−1, not future ones — otherwise it cheats by peeking at the answer. Method: after computing attention scores, set the upper-right triangle (future positions) to −∞; softmax turns them into 0 weights, so future tokens contribute nothing. Steps: ①Q, K, V come from the already-generated tokens (split into multiple heads); ②scores S = QKᵀ/√d_k (scaled dot-product); ③apply the mask: future positions become −∞ (keep the diagonal and lower-left); ④softmax normalises each row and future weights become 0; ⑤weighted sum of V; ⑥each head is computed separately, then concatenated and projected; ⑦add & norm follows. Note: the mask is only in decoder self-attention; encoder self-attention and cross-attention do not need it.

### 【样题 Q10 · 3分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** Transformer不像RNN顺序处理token，说一个优势和一个挑战。
**英文答案（English answer）：** Advantage: parallel processing. All tokens are processed at once, so computation is easily parallelised on GPUs, making training on large datasets much faster than sequential RNNs. Challenge: loss of word order information. Because there is no recurrence, the model has no innate sense of order, so explicit positional encodings must be added.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 优势是并行计算，所有token同时处理，能在GPU上轻松并行，海量数据训练比串行RNN快得多。挑战是丢失位置信息，没有循环结构，模型本身分不清词序，必须显式加位置编码。

## C.11 解码算法（大纲第9周）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 贪心和束搜索的区别？束宽 k 太大/太小各有什么问题？Top-p 是什么？

**中文：** 贪心解码每步选概率最高的词，快但一步错步步错，可能一直重复某个词。束搜索设置束宽k，每步保留k个最高概率候选序列，最后选总概率最高的；k等于1时就是贪心，k太大容易出无关句子。Top-k采样选概率前k个词重新归一化再随机抽。Top-p采样选到累计概率超过90%的词再采样。温度采样在softmax前用温度参数改变概率分布的陡峭程度，配合Top-k或Top-p用。
**English:** Greedy decoding picks the most probable token each step — fast, but one error leads to more errors and it may repeat words. Beam search keeps the k most probable candidate sequences each step and finally picks the highest total probability one; k=1 equals greedy, too-large k gives irrelevant sentences. Top-k sampling picks the top-k tokens, re-normalises and samples. Top-p sampling samples from tokens whose cumulative probability exceeds 90%. Temperature scales the softmax distribution before sampling, usually combined with top-k/top-p.

## C.12 语言模型评估（大纲第9周）  ★★

> **可能考法（预测）：** ①概念简答：什么是困惑度？越低越好吗？局限？②对比简答：BLEU 和 BERTScore 区别？为什么只看 1-gram 不合理？③串联题（大纲原话）：BERTScore 用上下文嵌入算余弦相似度，和第11周 CLIP 图文相似度底层思想共通——这个类比要能自己说。④局限题：自动指标算不出流畅度/风格/多样性，所以还要人工评估、或让大模型当裁判（大模型打分不稳定）。⑤**防计算题**：给候选译文+参考译文，手算 BLEU（公式在下方）。

**中文：** 困惑度Perplexity衡量模型混乱程度，数值越低越好，但高低不代表人类眼里文本质量好坏。BLEU和ROUGE基于n-gram重合：1-gram只看词在不在（复读机也高分，不合理），3-4 gram要求连续词按顺序出现（更严格）。BERTScore把文本送进模型拿上下文token嵌入，两两算余弦相似度，得到精确率和召回率。自动指标有局限，流畅度、风格、多样性需要人工评估，也可以让大模型打分但结果不稳定。
**English:** Perplexity measures how confused the model is; lower is better, but it does not equal human-judged quality. BLEU/ROUGE measure n-gram overlap: 1-gram only checks whether words appear (a parrot can score high — unreasonable); 3-4 gram requires words in order (stricter). BERTScore sends texts into a model, gets contextual token embeddings, and computes pairwise cosine similarity into precision and recall. Automatic metrics are limited: fluency, style and diversity need human evaluation; LLM judges are unstable too.

**中文（BLEU 怎么手算，防计算题）：** BLEU 衡量候选译文和参考译文的 n-gram 重合度。两步：①算 n-gram 精确率：数候选里有多少个 n-gram 在参考里出现（同一个 n-gram 最多按参考里出现的次数计，叫截段计数），除以候选 n-gram 总数；②长度惩罚 BP：候选比参考短就罚分（BP=e^(1−候选长度/参考长度)），不短就不罚。最后 BLEU = BP × 各 n-gram 精确率的几何平均。例子（1-gram）：候选 "the cat" 对参考 "the cat sat"，候选两个词都在参考里→精确率 2/2=1，但候选更短要乘 BP。
**English (how to compute BLEU by hand, in case of a calculation):** BLEU measures n-gram overlap between the candidate and the reference. Steps: ①n-gram precision: count how many candidate n-grams appear in the reference (clipped, so a repeated n-gram is counted at most its reference count), then divide by the total number of candidate n-grams; ②brevity penalty BP: if the candidate is shorter than the reference, penalise with BP = e^(1 − candidate_length/reference_length); otherwise BP = 1. Finally BLEU = BP × geometric mean of the precisions. Example (1-gram): candidate "the cat" vs reference "the cat sat": both candidate words appear in the reference → precision = 2/2 = 1, but the candidate is shorter, so multiply by BP.

# Part D — Pretrained Models and LLMs 预训练模型与大模型（16分，Q11 Q12 Q13 Q14）

> **注意：** 这一整块（Teacher Forcing、BERT、GPT、RLHF、上下文学习、PEFT、现代组件、多模态）**2021年卷子上完全没有考**，是 2026 年大纲新增的核心重点——样题 Q11-Q14 全考这块，占 16 分，是四个 Part 里分值最高的，**必须重点背**。

## D.1 预训练 & 分词  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 为什么先预训练再微调？BPE 怎么一步步合并词？

**中文：** 预训练是在一个已知任务上先训练神经网络，再在新的下游任务上微调，复用训练好的权重，以前是随机初始化，现在是复用预训练权重。为了解决语料库外的词OOV，后来用BPE字节对编码：先从单字符词汇表开始，找最常见的相邻字符组合加为子词，用子词替换字符对，重复直到词表达到期望大小。
**English:** Pre-training trains a network on a known task first, then fine-tunes it on a new downstream task, reusing the trained weights instead of random initialisation. To handle out-of-vocabulary words, BPE is used: start from a vocabulary of single characters, merge the most frequent adjacent character pairs into subwords, replace and repeat until the vocabulary reaches the target size.

## D.2 seq2seq 编解码器 & Teacher Forcing  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** Teacher Forcing 是什么？训练时喂什么、生成时喂什么？（样题 Q11）

**中文：** 编码器把输入变成向量，解码器逐词生成输出，生成时给BOS表示开始，出现EOS时停止。Teacher Forcing的意思是：训练时不管模型上一步预测了什么，下一步输入一律换成真实答案标签，这样训练收敛快、稳定；但推理时没有标准答案，模型只能用自己上一步的输出，一旦预测错，错误会一路累积，这叫曝光偏差。
**English:** The encoder turns the input into vectors; the decoder generates output word by word, starting with BOS and stopping at EOS. Teacher forcing: during training, no matter what the model predicted in the previous step, the next input is always the ground-truth label, which speeds convergence and stabilises training. At inference there is no ground truth, so the model must feed its own previous prediction; once it is wrong, errors accumulate — exposure bias.

### 【样题 Q11 · 3分 · 必背答案】

**问题（Question）：** 为什么teacher forcing训练时有益，但给没见过的文档生成摘要时不能用？举例。
**英文答案（English answer）：** In training, teacher forcing feeds the ground-truth token as the next input, which speeds convergence and stabilises training. At inference there is no ground-truth summary for unseen documents, so the model must feed its own previous prediction (autoregressive). Example: gold summary "The dog barked". Training: if the model predicts "The cat", we still feed the true word "dog" next. Inference: if it predicts "The cat", nothing fixes it and it keeps generating "The cat meowed", so errors accumulate (exposure bias).
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 训练时teacher forcing喂真实token当下一步输入，加速收敛、训练稳定。推理时对新文档没有标准答案摘要，模型只能用自己上一步的预测。例子：标准答案"The dog barked"。训练时就算模型猜"The cat"，下一步还是喂真的"dog"；推理时一旦输出"The cat"没人纠正，会接着生成"The cat meowed"，一步错步步错，这就是曝光偏差。

## D.3 ELMo & GPT1 & BERT  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** ELMo/GPT1/BERT 各怎么训练？MLM 和 NSP 各是什么？CLS/SEP 干什么？（样题 Q12）

**中文：** ELMo是动态词向量，同一个词在不同上下文有不同的向量，用双向LSTM生成。GPT1只需要预测下一个词，所以舍弃编码器只留解码器，用掩码自注意力，只能从左向右学习。BERT是双向Transformer，用两套Transformer，目标是"一套架构完成大多数任务"。BERT的输入有CLS标记（放开头，聚合整句语义）和SEP标记（分隔符），还用段嵌入区分两个句子。分词用WordPiece，比BPE多算关联分数。
**English:** ELMo gives dynamic word vectors — the same word has different vectors in different contexts, generated by a bidirectional LSTM. GPT1 only needs to predict the next word, so it keeps only the decoder with masked self-attention, learning left to right. BERT is a bidirectional Transformer (two Transformer blocks); the goal is one architecture for most tasks. BERT's input uses [CLS] (at the start, aggregates the whole sentence) and [SEP] (separator), plus segment embeddings to distinguish two sentences. Tokenisation uses WordPiece, which also computes association scores.

**中文（CLS/SEP 是干嘛的、用于训练什么，大纲第6周"记住各自用途"）：** CLS 和 SEP 不是知识，是输入格式的"配件"。CLS 放句首，它的最终向量=整句浓缩，分类信号从它取；SEP 是句子分界，把句A句B切开，配段嵌入告诉模型哪段是哪段。训练时它俩出现在两个预训练任务里：MLM（盖住词猜原词）输入是"[CLS] 句子 [SEP]"，被盖住的词位要预测；NSP（判断句B是不是句A的下一句）输入是"[CLS] 句A [SEP] 句B [SEP]"，模型看 CLS 位置的输出做二分类。微调分类任务同样取 CLS。一句话：CLS=整句代表（供分类/NSP取信号），SEP=句子分界（句子对输入格式）。
**English (what CLS/SEP are for in training, Week 6 "remember their uses"):** CLS and SEP are input-format tokens, not learned knowledge. CLS sits at the start; its final vector summarises the whole sentence and is the signal for classification. SEP is the boundary between sentences; segment embeddings tell BERT which tokens belong to sentence A and which to B. In pre-training they appear in both tasks: MLM uses "[CLS] sentence [SEP]" and every masked position is predicted; NSP uses "[CLS] A [SEP] B [SEP]" and the model reads the CLS output to decide whether B is the next sentence of A. Fine-tuning classification also uses CLS. In short: CLS = sentence summary (signal for classification/NSP), SEP = sentence boundary (format for sentence pairs).

**中文（BERT两个预训练任务）：** MLM掩码语言模型随机盖住一部分词让模型预测，但不能100%用掩码，否则预训练和微调分布不匹配；NSP下一句预测判断两句话是不是原文连续的两句。微调时预训练权重全保留，只换输出层：分类任务取CLS的向量加输出层，抽取式问答预测答案起止位置，NER每个token输出标签。
**English (BERT's two pre-training tasks):** MLM randomly masks some tokens and asks the model to predict them; it must not use 100% mask, otherwise the pre-training and fine-tuning distributions mismatch. NSP predicts whether sentence B follows sentence A. For fine-tuning, the pre-trained weights stay and only the output layer changes: classification uses the [CLS] vector; extractive QA predicts start/end positions; NER labels every token.

### 【样题 Q12 · 4分 · 必背答案（BERT三分类情感）】

**问题（Question）：** 用预训练BERT做三分类情感（积极/消极/中立），怎么适配？取哪个输出、加什么组件？
**英文答案（English answer）：** Input: prepend [CLS] at the start → "[CLS] text [SEP]". Pass through BERT and extract the final hidden vector of the [CLS] token, which is the aggregated summary of the whole sentence. Add a Linear/Dense classification head projecting the [CLS] vector (e.g. 768-dim) to 3 outputs, then a Softmax layer to convert logits into probabilities summing to 1. Fine-tune the linear layer (and optionally BERT) with cross-entropy loss on the labelled sentiment dataset.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** 输入开头加CLS，变成"[CLS] 文本 [SEP]"。过BERT后取CLS token的最终隐藏向量，它聚合了整句语义。加一个线性分类头，把CLS向量（如768维）映射到3个输出，再接Softmax把logits转成和为1的概率。用交叉熵损失在带标签情感数据集上微调，可以只微调线性层，也可以顺带微调BERT。

**中文（BERT家族，了解即可）：** RoBERTa用更多数据和轮次训练；XLNet用相对位置；ALBERT压缩词嵌入；ELECTRA用噪声词判断是否被改过；Longformer用滑动窗口注意力处理超长文本；ViLBERT是图文双Transformer。
**English (BERT family, just know the names):** RoBERTa trains longer on more data; XLNet uses relative positions; ALBERT compresses embeddings; ELECTRA judges whether a word was replaced by noise; Longformer uses sliding-window attention for long texts; ViLBERT is a vision-language dual Transformer.

## D.4 GPT 系列 & RLHF（大纲第9周）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** GPT 为什么有效？RLHF 三步各干什么？（样题 Q13）

**中文：** GPT1只留解码器，去掉编码器和交叉注意力。GPT2层归一化位置改变、模型更大。GPT3引入稀疏注意力（局部注意力）。GPT为什么有效：它唯一的目标是学习共现模式，把句子里每个词和所有其他词比较，重新加权词嵌入包含上下文相关性，并增大模型规模。RLHF是让大模型输出对齐人类偏好。
**English:** GPT1 keeps only the decoder, removing the encoder and cross-attention. GPT2 moves layer normalisation and is much larger. GPT3 introduces sparse (local) attention. Why GPT works: its only goal is to learn co-occurrence patterns — each word is compared with every other word, embeddings are re-weighted with context relevance, and the model scale grows. RLHF aligns LLM outputs with human preferences.

### 【样题 Q13 · 4分 · 必背答案（RLHF三步）】

**问题（Question）：** 什么是RLHF？简述主要步骤。
**英文答案（English answer）：** RLHF aligns language models with human preferences, making outputs more helpful, honest and harmless. Steps: 1) Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): fine-tune a base LLM on high-quality human prompt-response pairs to build instruction-following. 2) Reward model training: generate several outputs for prompts, human annotators rank them, train a reward model to predict preference scores. 3) Reinforcement learning (PPO): fine-tune the SFT model using PPO, using the reward model's scores as the reinforcement signal.
**中文答案（Chinese answer）：** RLHF让大模型输出对齐人类偏好，更实用、诚实、无害。三步：①SFT监督微调，用高质量人类提示-回答对微调底座模型，建立指令遵循能力；②奖励模型训练，对提示词生成多条回复，人工排序，训练奖励模型预测偏好分数；③PPO强化学习，用奖励模型的分数当强化信号，用PPO微调SFT模型。

**中文（RLHF补充，理解即可）：** 大纲把RLHF拆成四步：有监督微调、生成多条回复让人类排序打分、训练奖励模型、PPO更新模型，最后优化对话自然度。PPO要加KL惩罚项，防止奖励劫持——模型为了拿高分输出超长重复的内容。
**English (RLHF details):** The syllabus splits it into four steps: SFT, generate multiple replies and rank them, train the reward model, update with PPO, then optimise conversation quality. PPO adds a KL penalty to prevent reward hacking — the model producing long repetitive output just to score high.

## D.5 上下文学习 & 思维链（大纲第10周）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** zero-shot / one-shot / few-shot 区别？思维链为什么只在大模型上有效？

**中文：** 上下文学习是预训练语言模型通过在prompt里给几个输入-输出示例就完成任务的能力，不需要梯度训练、不更新权重。分三类：zero-shot不给例子，one-shot给1个，few-shot给几个。大模型上少样本效果好于零样本，小模型相反。思维链是对复杂推理任务先写出思考步骤再给答案，只在大模型上效果好。上下文学习的缺点：受上下文窗口限制，提示词敏感。
**English:** In-context learning: a pretrained LLM completes a task from a few examples in the prompt, with NO gradient training and NO weight updates. Types: zero-shot (no examples), one-shot (1 example), few-shot (several). On large models, few-shot beats zero-shot; on small models the opposite. Chain-of-thought writes reasoning steps before the answer; it works only on large models. Drawbacks: limited by the context window and sensitive to the prompt wording.

**中文（示例怎么选，第10讲"影响示例质量的因素"，可能考）：** ①相关性：和测试输入越像越有用；②多样性：示例要覆盖各种可能的输入输出；③清晰度：输入-输出关系要明确没歧义；④正确性：标签错了会误导模型；⑤排序：放在 prompt 末尾的示例影响最强。例子：餐厅评论 "The steak was cooked perfectly → Positive"、"Never coming back here again → Negative"。
**English (how to choose examples, Lecture 10 "factors that influence example quality"):** ①Relevance: examples similar to the test input help more; ②Diversity: cover the range of possible inputs and outputs; ③Clarity: unambiguous input-output relationships; ④Correctness: wrong labels mislead the model; ⑤Ordering: examples at the end of the prompt have the strongest influence. Example: "The steak was cooked perfectly → Positive", "Never coming back here again → Negative".

## D.6 微调 & PEFT 参数高效微调（大纲第8、10周）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** 预训练微调/指令微调/上下文学习怎么区分？Prompt Tuning 和 Prefix Tuning 区别？LoRA 为什么省参数？

**中文（三种学习方式怎么区分，大纲第10周考点）：** ①预训练微调：先在超大无标注语料上学通用语言（预训练），再用带标签的小任务数据更新权重（微调，换输出层）。例子：BERT 在维基百科预训练，再用情感标注数据微调做情感分类（样题Q12就是）。②指令微调：是微调的一种，但数据是"指令-回答"配对，目标是让模型听懂指令、变成听话的助手。例子：Alpaca 用指令-回答对微调 Llama；ChatGPT 的 RLHF 第一步 SFT 就是指令微调。③上下文学习：不更新任何权重，在 prompt 里给几个输入-输出示例，模型现场推理完成任务。例子：prompt 里先给"你好→Hello"，再让模型翻译"谢谢→____"。什么时候用哪种：有标注数据、要稳定效果→预训练微调；想让模型当助手、有指令数据→指令微调；快速试一下、没算力、任务老变→上下文学习。一句话：微调=更新权重学任务，指令微调=更新权重学听话，上下文学习=不更新权重现场发挥。
**English (how to tell the three apart, Week 10):** ①Pre-training + fine-tuning: pre-train on a large unlabeled corpus (general language), then fine-tune on labelled task data (update weights, new output head). Example: BERT pre-trained on Wikipedia → fine-tuned on sentiment data (like sample Q12). ②Instruction tuning: a special fine-tuning whose data are (instruction, response) pairs; the goal is to make the model follow instructions like an assistant. Example: Alpaca fine-tunes Llama on instruction-response pairs; step 1 of RLHF (SFT) is instruction tuning. ③In-context learning: no weight update; put a few input-output examples in the prompt and the model infers the task on the spot. Example: give "你好 → Hello" in the prompt, then ask the model to translate "谢谢 → ___". When to use which: labelled data + stable performance → fine-tuning; want an assistant + instruction data → instruction tuning; quick trial / no compute / changing tasks → in-context learning. In short: fine-tuning updates weights to learn a task; instruction tuning updates weights to follow instructions; in-context learning updates nothing.

**中文（老师讲义三个案例，第10讲，考试让举例子直接抄）：** Case 1：预训练 BERT 在带标签的法律文档上微调做分类 → 预训练微调（要很多任务样本，每个任务一个专用模型）。Case 2：给 LLM 几个"非正式→正式"改写示例，再让它改写一个新句子 → 上下文学习（few-shot，不改权重）。Case 3：预训练 Llama 用大量"自然语言指令+预期回答"的样本微调 → 指令微调（学会听懂指令）。**怎么选：** 上下文学习优点=零训练、提示工程（如思维链）容易提性能、结果可解释；缺点=受上下文窗口限制、提示敏感、不持久、复杂任务举几个例子教不会、只有大模型才有明显提升——所以复杂任务还得回到微调。而全微调又有三大局限（算力随模型涨、多任务存储叠加、灾难性遗忘），所以用 PEFT 折中。
**English (three cases from Lecture 10, copy for the exam):** Case 1: a pretrained BERT is fine-tuned on labelled legal documents to classify them → pre-train + fine-tune (needs many task-specific examples; one specialized model per task). Case 2: give an LLM a few informal→formal rewriting examples, then ask it to rewrite a new sentence → in-context learning (few-shot, no weight update). Case 3: pretrained Llama is fine-tuned on many task samples of (natural-language instruction, expected response) → instruction tuning (learns to follow instructions). How to choose: ICL pros = weight-free, prompt engineering (e.g. CoT) easily boosts performance, explainable results; cons = limited by context window, prompt-sensitive, no permanent learning, too complex to demo in a few examples, gains only in large models — so go back to tuning for complex tasks. Full fine-tuning also has three limits (cost scales with model size, storage compounds across tasks, catastrophic forgetting), hence PEFT as a compromise.

**中文：** 全量微调有三个缺点：计算成本高、存储开销大（每个任务存一份完整模型）、灾难性遗忘（学新任务忘了预训练通用知识）。所以用参数高效微调PEFT，只训练少量参数。PEFT分三类：参数层面改造是LoRA（冻结原始权重，新增两个很小的可训练低秩矩阵）；输入层面改造是Prompt Tuning（在输入前加软提示）和Prefix Tuning（在K、V上加前缀）；函数层面改造是Adapter（在网络中插入小模块）。
**English:** Full fine-tuning has three drawbacks: high computation cost, high storage (a full model per task), and catastrophic forgetting (losing general knowledge when learning a new task). So PEFT trains only a small number of parameters. Three categories: parameter-level — LoRA (freeze the original weights, add two small trainable low-rank matrices); input-level — Prompt Tuning (add a soft prompt before the input) and Prefix Tuning (add prefixes to K and V); function-level — Adapter (insert small modules into the network).

**中文（PEFT 细节，第10讲）：** LoRA：冻结预训练权重 W₀，新增两个很小的低秩矩阵 B 和 A，前向算 h = W₀x + BAx（两路并行算完相加），只训 B、A；ΔW = B·A，秩 r 要远小于矩阵维度（r≪min(d,k)）。例子：RoBERTa-base 全微调要训 1.25 亿参数，LoRA 只训 0.3M，性能几乎一样。Adapter：往模型里插小模块（瓶颈结构：先降维→激活→升维），一般放在多头注意力之后和/或 FFN 之后。IA³：学几个重缩放向量 l，乘到注意力的 K/V 和 FFN 的中间激活上，比 LoRA 更省参数。
**English (PEFT details, Lecture 10):** LoRA: freeze W₀ and add two small low-rank matrices B and A; forward computes h = W₀x + BAx (two parallel paths summed); only B and A are trained; ΔW = B·A with rank r ≪ min(d,k). Example: full fine-tuning of RoBERTa-base trains 125M parameters; LoRA trains only 0.3M with nearly the same performance. Adapter: insert small modules into the model (bottleneck: down-project → activation → up-project), usually after multi-head attention and/or after the FFN. IA³: learn a few rescaling vectors l and multiply them into the K/V of attention and the FFN activations — even more parameter-efficient than LoRA.

**中文（Prompt Tuning vs Prefix Tuning，大纲第10周点名，大白话）：** 两个都属于"输入层面改造"，都不改模型主体、只训练极少量参数，加的都是可训练的向量（软提示 soft prompt，不是真词）。区别在加在哪：Prompt Tuning 只在**最前面（输入嵌入层）**加一串可训练的"软提示"向量——模型主体冻结，只训这串向量，让模型顺着提示的意思干活（比如情感分类往积极/消极方向走）。Prefix Tuning 更进一步：给**每一层的 K 和 V 前面**都加可训练的前缀向量，把提示注入每层的注意力计算里，训练也只训这些前缀。区别一句话：Prompt Tuning 只在入口加一次提示（浅层），Prefix Tuning 每层 K/V 都加（深入每层注意力，表达力更强）。
**English (Prompt Tuning vs Prefix Tuning, named in Week 10):** Both are input-level PEFT: the model body is frozen and only a tiny number of parameters are trained; both use a soft prompt — trainable vectors, not real words. The difference is where the prompt is added. Prompt Tuning adds a trainable soft prompt only at the input embedding layer; the model body stays frozen and only these vectors are trained, steering the model toward the task (e.g. sentiment classification). Prefix Tuning goes further: it adds trainable prefix vectors to the K and V of EVERY layer, injecting the prompt into the attention computation; only these prefixes are trained. In short: Prompt Tuning adds the prompt once at the input (shallow); Prefix Tuning adds prefixes at every layer's K/V (deeper and more expressive).

## D.7 现代大模型组件（大纲第11周）  ★★
> **考试可能这样问：** Llama 比 GPT 改了哪四样？RoPE / RMS Norm / GQA / SwiGLU / MoE 各一句话原理？

**中文：** Llama的设计哲学是不发明新组件，挑选已被验证有效的组件组合起来。和GPT相比有四项变化：位置编码用RoPE，归一化用RMS Norm，归一化位置改到层前（Pre-Norm），激活函数换SwiGLU。RMS Norm省去中心化只做缩放，更省算力。RoPE在每层注意力对Q、K做旋转再点积，只看相对位置。GQA分组查询注意力把注意力头分组，同组共用KV，省内存效果接近。MoE混合专家把FFN拆成多个专家，路由器选部分专家激活。
**English:** Llama's philosophy: do not invent new components, combine components already proven effective. Four changes vs GPT: RoPE positional encoding, RMS Norm, Pre-Norm placement, SwiGLU activation. RMS Norm removes centering and only scales — cheaper. RoPE rotates Q and K in each attention layer before the dot product, so only relative positions matter. GQA groups heads and shares KV within a group — saves memory with quality close to full attention. MoE splits FFN into experts; a router activates only some experts per token.

**中文（六个组件一句话原理，大纲第11周点名，大白话）：** ①RoPE 旋转位置编码：位置不靠"加"，而是把每个 token 的 Q 和 K 按位置旋转一定角度后再点积，旋转后的点积只反映两词的相对距离——所以叫"只看相对位置"。②RMS Norm：Layer Norm 要做"减均值+缩放"，RMS Norm 省掉减均值、只做缩放，算得快、效果几乎一样。③Pre-Norm：把归一化放在子层计算之前，让梯度沿残差通路更顺畅，训练更稳（Llama 用它）。④SwiGLU 激活：公式 y=x·Sigmoid(x)，是"自门控"——用 sigmoid 给 x 自己当开关，保留大部分正值、放少量负值，曲线比 ReLU 平滑，是 Llama/Qwen/Mistral 的标配。⑤GQA 分组查询注意力：标准注意力每头独立的 K/V（贵），MQA 全部头共用一组 K/V（省内存但效果降），GQA 是折中——把注意力头分组，组内共用 K/V，省内存且效果接近标准注意力（Llama 用的就是 GQA）。⑥MoE 混合专家：把 FFN 换成多个"专家"（各自独立的 FFN），路由器 router 给每个 token 挑 top-k 个专家激活；总参数量很大，但每次只算少数专家，省算力（Mixtral 就是）。一句话背：RoPE 管相对位置、RMS 省中心化、Pre-Norm 稳训练、SwiGLU 自门控、GQA 分组省 KV、MoE 路由挑专家。
**English (one-line principles, Week 11):** ①RoPE: instead of adding position, rotate each token's Q and K by an angle based on its position before the dot product, so the score reflects only relative distance. ②RMS Norm: Layer Norm does "subtract mean + scale"; RMS Norm removes the mean subtraction and only scales — faster with nearly the same quality. ③Pre-Norm: normalise BEFORE the sublayer, keeping gradients flowing through the residual path and stabilising training (used by Llama). ④SwiGLU: y = x·Sigmoid(x) — self-gating: sigmoid acts as a switch for x itself, keeping most positive values and allowing a little negative, smoother than ReLU; standard in Llama/Qwen/Mistral. ⑤GQA: standard attention gives each head its own K/V (expensive); MQA shares one K/V across all heads (cheap but weaker); GQA groups heads and shares K/V within each group — saves memory with quality close to full attention (Llama uses GQA). ⑥MoE: replace FFN with multiple "experts"; a router picks the top-k experts per token, so the total parameter count is huge but only a few experts are computed — cheaper (e.g. Mixtral). One-line memory: RoPE for relative position, RMS saves centering, Pre-Norm stabilises training, SwiGLU self-gates, GQA shares KV by group, MoE routes experts.

## D.8 多模态 & 视觉（大纲第11周）  ★★★
> **考试可能这样问：** CLIP 怎么训练（图文匹配）？ViT 怎么把图片当句子？（样题 Q14 串联）

**中文：** ViT把图片切成patch块当成token序列，加绝对位置送进Transformer。CLIP是双编码器，图像编码器加文本编码器，用图文匹配训练，最大化匹配图文相似度、最小化不匹配，支持零样本图像分类。LLaVA冻结CLIP视觉编码器和LLM，只训练中间的投影层，把图像patch特征转成文本对齐的视觉token，分特征对齐预训练和视觉指令微调两阶段。
**English:** ViT splits an image into patches, treats them as a token sequence with absolute positions, and feeds them into a Transformer. CLIP is a dual encoder (image + text) trained with image-text matching: maximise similarity for matched pairs, minimise for unmatched; it supports zero-shot image classification. LLaVA freezes the CLIP vision encoder and the LLM, training only the projection layer to turn image patch features into text-aligned visual tokens, in two stages: feature-alignment pre-training and visual instruction tuning.

## D.9 QA 系统 & 机器翻译（笔记有、样题没直接考，了解）  ★
